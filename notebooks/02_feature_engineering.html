Feature Significance Assessment¶

A statistical feature significance analysis is performed to evaluate the relationship between clinical biomarkers and diagnosis categories. This assessment helps identify the variables with the strongest discriminatory potential before model development.

In [10]:
from sklearn.feature_selection import f_classif

X = df[
    [
        "age",
        "plasma_C",
        "creatinine",
        "LYVE1",
        "REG1B",
        "TFF1",
        "REG1A",
        "CA19_9",
        "CEA",
        "bilirubin",
        "glucose",
        "urine_volume",
        "urine_pH"
    ]
]

y = df["diagnosis"]

f_scores, p_values = f_classif(
    X, 
    y
)

feature_scores = pd.DataFrame({
    "Feature": X.columns,
    "F_score": f_scores,
    "p_value": p_values
})


feature_scores = (
    feature_scores
    .sort_values(
        by="F_score",
        ascending=False
    )
)

Extreme biomarker values were retained because they are likely to reflect genuine clinical variation rather than measurement errors. Removing these observations could reduce the model's ability to identify high-risk patients.

Multicollinearity Assessment¶

The relationships between biomarkers are evaluated to identify potential redundancy among clinical variables. Variance Inflation Factor (VIF) is used to assess whether multiple biomarkers capture similar information.

In [15]:
from statsmodels.stats.outliers_influence import variance_inflation_factor

X_vif = df[
    [
        "age",
        "plasma_C",
        "creatinine",
        "LYVE1",
        "REG1B",
        "TFF1",
        "REG1A",
        "CA19_9",
        "CEA",
        "bilirubin",
        "glucose",
        "urine_volume",
        "urine_pH"
    ]
]

vif = pd.DataFrame()

vif["Feature"] = X_vif.columns

vif["VIF"] = [
    variance_inflation_factor(
        X_vif.values,
        i
    )
    for i in range(len(X_vif.columns))
]

vif.sort_values(
    by="VIF",
    ascending=False
)

Findings¶

No evidence of problematic multicollinearity was identified among the clinical biomarkers. These results indicate that the biomarkers provide largely complementary information and can be retained for modelling without introducing severe redundancy.

Feature Selection and Validation Summary¶

The feature validation process was conducted to assess the usefulness and reliability of the available clinical biomarkers. Several biomarkers, particularly REG1A, REG1B, TFF1, CA19-9 and CEA, exhibited strong relationships with diagnosis status and clear separation between patient groups Outlier analysis showed that extreme biomarker values were predominantly associated with pancreatic cancer patients and are therefore considered clinically meaningful rather than erroneous measurements Multicollinearity assessment indicated low redundancy among variables, with all Variance Inflation Factor (VIF) values remaining below commonly accepted thresholds. Consequently, all available biomarkers were retained for model development.
